Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our matching process, therapist qualifications, and privacy.

When you complete our 3-minute confidential questionnaire, we assess your clinical needs, cultural preferences, communication style, and scheduling availability. Our clinical matching engine then pairs you with a therapist licensed in your state whose specialty aligns with your goals. Over 98% of clients love their initial match!

Yes, absolutely. Every therapist on Drozo is an independently licensed, state-board certified mental health practitioner (such as a PsyD, PhD, LMFT, LCSW, or LPCC). Each therapist holds a Master’s or Doctorate degree, has completed at least 3 years and 1,000+ hours of supervised clinical experience, and has passed extensive background screening.

You can connect directly through your private Drozo portal on your computer, tablet, or smartphone without downloading clunky software. You choose whether each live session is conducted via high-definition video, audio-only phone call, or synchronous live messaging. You also have 24/7 access to your secure chatroom to leave notes anytime.

Drozo directly accepts most major Health Savings Accounts (HSA) and Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA) cards. While we are an out-of-network provider for commercial health insurance to guarantee total privacy and eliminate arbitrary insurer session caps, we provide itemized superbills upon request which many clients successfully submit for partial insurance reimbursement.

Therapy is deeply personal, and chemistry matters. If you ever feel your matched therapist isn’t the right fit, you can switch with a single click inside your account dashboard at any point during your subscription—with zero awkward conversations and zero additional fees.

Uncompromising confidentiality is our highest ethical priority. All video streams and messaging rooms are protected with banking-grade 256-bit SSL encryption. We are 100% HIPAA compliant, our servers are guarded around the clock, and we adhere to a strict policy never to sell or disclose patient data under any commercial circumstances.
